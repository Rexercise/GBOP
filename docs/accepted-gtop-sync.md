## Verification and limits

Synthetic tests cover current V1 and V2 reports, missing paired evidence,
corrections, repeated save, fresh-session recall, distinct lexical labels,
Fang tier/boundaries, KOD nature, unchanged allocations/history, H4 names,
protection wording, and unique revision IDs. They are not real trade outcomes.

Do not equate a local file, test, published library, or repository merge with a
verified running GBOP/indicator. Final full-suite, release SHA, runtime prompt
and authenticated live checks belong in the coordinated release receipt.

## Named H4 journal references

`range_reference.py` resolves all six accepted New York H4 windows and their
supported spoken/legacy aliases without claiming a native Chart H4 candle.
TCT is dated by its 21:00 opening and ends at 01:00 on the following New York
date. Completed ordinary windows require complete matching broker-bar coverage;
forming windows, mismatched feeds and missing bars remain unverified. No schema,
price-action rule, execution or historical data change is made.

RCT on a daylight-saving transition has three or five elapsed hours despite its
01:00–05:00 wall-clock bounds. The existing fixed-duration H4 reader cannot verify
that special window; GBOP returns `named_range_clock_transition` without reading
or fabricating an H4 candle. Accurate local bounds remain in the response.

Focused synthetic checks cover aliases, all six reconstructed windows, TCT
midnight/date boundaries, relative dates, summer/winter offsets, DST fail-closed,
native/feed/timeframe conflicts, missing coverage and forming references.
