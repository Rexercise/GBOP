# GBOP knows. The Butterfly Tool shows.

The shared rulebook is the authority. For a selected TradingView chart, its actual candles and Butterfly's interpretation are the reference. An MT5 result is a separate observation, even when the market names match.

## What the candidate adds

The board leads with the focused ranges and their existing chart variant labels, followed by bounds, status/objective and assigned Model 1/CISD/Super Soup evidence. **Other context** lists each available range separately beneath the layer counts. Every range name and variant is red, including monthly, weekly, daily, H4 and H1; each context entry has its own bounds/status/objective hover. Full source chronology and confirmation times remain in tooltips. Developing ranges are marked **source close pending**, including a previous monthly range swept during the current unclosed month. Multiple parents remain listed, oldest first. Six-month and twelve-month ranges can appear in the context summary even though the shared rulebook has no assigned Model 1 timeframe for them. Their detailed execution evidence stays explicitly unmapped.

**Chart parent detail** now leads with visible H1 shift ranges on an hourly chart, including their Dayshift/Nightshift identity and the same Potential/Completed variant label used on the range drawing. Their assigned evidence remains M5; this presentation choice does not change the shared timeframe mapping. If no visible H1 range is available, hourly detail follows its daily parent, then the highest available mapped context. Other relationships remain M15 → H4/M15; M5 → H1/M5; H4 → weekly/H4; daily → monthly/daily. A visible developing monthly range keeps monthly detail on a daily chart; it is not displaced by an older confirmed quarterly range. **Highest relevant timeframe** and **Explicit parent layer** override automatic selection. An exact opening is also available. No option silently enables another range layer or promotes an untouched hourly candle. All surviving shift ranges remain independently listed; a new shift does not invalidate an older range.

Monthly detail shows the range's month and bounds, its separate source-close status, the pending objective, and closed daily Model 1/CISD/Super Soup evidence where available. A daily event cannot confirm or invalidate the monthly parent. Developing parents carry `source_close_pending` and unverified source coverage in chart captures; GBOP preserves that distinction and never promotes an unverified overview to confirmed relevance.

Every available relevant parent in the focused layer is checked independently. One Model 1, CISD or independently established Super Soup candle can link to several parents, but only if it qualifies against each. Shared events have one marker with the linked parent names and separate hover details. Parent objectives and own-timeframe validity remain separate. Captures retain every focused parent and the reported overview, plus a shared assigned-candle stream.

An incomplete prefix keeps the retained Model 1's **first identity unverified** and whole-window coverage incomplete. It does not suppress a later CISD witnessed in uninterrupted closed assigned candles from that retained Model 1 onward. The panel may therefore show **first unverified** alongside **CISD confirmed · Model 1 validated**. GBOP explains the same scope. A gap after the retained Model 1 still blocks a new CISD claim; exact boundary closes and forming candles still do not confirm. This distinction does not establish Super Soup or confirm the parent source candle.

The normal board never falls back automatically to a retired range. Deliberate historical detail requires **History** and **Review historical evidence**, plus the explicit layer or opening. Turning History off excludes retired detail and its callouts. Every drawing pass deletes the prior evidence labels before rebuilding the eligible current set. Changing chart timeframe changes the visible layers; it does not itself invalidate a source range. State is reconstructed from the available source history, not unlimited stored history.

Candles are named by **opening time**, with the confirming close retained in hover detail. **Model 1** is spelled out to distinguish it from M1. An established Super Soup is described by its cleanliness and parent function separately, such as **Super Soup · Degraded · Parent objective delivered**. A strict re-purge of its original wick degrades cleanliness even without an outside close; an exact touch does not. A Model 1 outside close inside that wick is a separate CRT fact. Hover shows excursion beyond the Soup wick in points and as a clearly named percentage of the original Model 1 range, separate Model 1-boundary extension, observation window and any coverage/order limits. This is setup behavior, not an assumed entry or trade result.

**Range boxes** hides only range drawings, their labels and DOL lines. Selected ranges, variants, source calculations, evidence callouts and captures remain available through their own controls. The evidence panel highlights each range name and its play/variant together in red; prices and status stay neutral. Red is emphasis, not a bearish-direction claim.

**Quick display → Range spotlight** numbers the panel's ranges. Set **Range number** to the bracketed number beside the desired name. The selected title stays red, becomes bold, and receives a gold background and arrow; its eligible box and DOL receive a gold outline. This works for both focused ranges and Other context. Selection follows the current panel order as ranges appear, retire or change with the chart timeframe; it is not a permanent timestamp pin. An unavailable number highlights nothing and shows a notice. The selected eligible box receives priority within the existing drawing limit. Range boxes off keeps the title selected while hiding all range drawings. Turning spotlight off restores normal styling. Qualification, objectives and GBOP captures are unaffected.

**Quick display** has independent **Show price-reading evidence** and **Mark the key evidence candles** switches. Directly below the master callout switch, **Model 1**, **CISD** and **Super Soup** each have their own checkbox on one row. Any combination can be shown; the master hides all callouts without clearing those choices. These switches affect candle labels only, leaving evidence-panel status, range validity and capture results intact. Return remains hover-only. **Sticky notes** defaults to top center with a transparent background (0% opacity). In **Watermark and sticky note → Note position**, choose any of nine chart positions. Competing panels move aside to keep the notes visible; nonblank saved rules appear when enabled. Existing saved opacity choices remain editable in **Note background**. The panel can be hidden while callouts remain, and vice versa. Range boxes/objectives and an independently enabled capture retain their own controls.

## Color a dated shift

Use **Color a dated shift → Shift date (YYYY-MM-DD)**, then choose **Dayshift 09–12**, **Nightshift 21–24**, **Human Max 00–09**, or **Human Max 12–21**. Each named row offers Default, Bullish, Bearish, Neutral and Custom with its own swatch. No dot matching is needed for this workflow. Times use the GTOP timezone.

The date is fixed and never rolls at midnight. Editing it intentionally moves this one dated annotation. Additional retained dates remain available in the existing advanced three-day annotations. Existing chart-picked dates/colors remain saved in **Advanced · saved chart-picked shift colors** and keep their original priority on overlap; setting a saved slot Off lets the dated choice replace it. **Custom shift colors** remains the independent master switch, and **Time ribbon** remains the visibility switch. The ribbon uses price coordinates; a chart set to **Scale price chart only** can place it below the visible area. Including indicator drawings in automatic scaling reveals the ribbon without changing its dates or colors.

## Use an actual chart capture

After installing this candidate and deploying its GBOP counterpart:

1. Keep the actual TradingView provider and symbol you use. Choose the evidence range/timeframe in Butterfly. The implementation does not hardcode OANDA or NAS100.
2. Enable **Export chart evidence in alert messages**. Create a TradingView alert for this indicator using **Any alert() function call**. It emits at a realtime chart candle's close when a closed selected range is available. A lower chart interval can produce a capture sooner without changing the selected parent timeframe.
3. Copy the complete JSON message from that alert. In GBOP Voice, open **Read my Butterfly chart**, paste it and choose **Use this chart capture**.
4. Ask GBOP about the selected chart. The exact validated capture accompanies backend voice requests. Broker market tools and broker prefetch are disabled for these chart requests. **Clear chart capture** restores ordinary broker-backed requests. Editing the text or signing out clears the selection; a late response cannot restore it.

TradingView alerts keep a saved copy of the script and its inputs. Recreate the alert after updating the script, symbol or relevant settings. See [TradingView's alert documentation](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/alerts/).

## What GBOP receives

Earlier `gtop.chart.v2` single-parent captures remain readable. Each `gtop.chart.v3` message includes the rules version/hash, actual TradingView symbol/provider, all focused parent identities, bounds, states and source definitions, the reported active-layer overview, timeframe mapping, closed-evidence cutoff, capture time, up to 192 actual closed assigned-timeframe OHLC candles, and Butterfly's displayed interpretation with event timestamps. Still-relevant parent identities may precede the retained candle window; their age alone no longer rejects the capture. Missing earlier candles still prevent verified primary identity or complete coverage. This does not expand the separate legacy broker-comparison retrieval window.

The optional `variant_display` preserves the exact chart label, including **Potential V3**, **Potential V1/V3**, **Completed V4**, or an explicit unclassified/pending status. GBOP treats it as indicator-reported context. It does not promote Potential into a completed variant or an entry. The existing `variant` field continues to carry completed single-code classification only. Model 1 identity, CISD validation and established Super Soup remain separate facts. A Model 1 outside close is recorded independently; only strict re-purge of the original Soup wick degrades Soup cleanliness, while parent delivery continues separately.

GBOP validates the envelope and candle consistency, checks local body Model 1 and strict full-extreme CISD using the shared predicates, and reuses its existing candle reviewer when the complete captured anchor and interval grid permit it. The indicator-reported lifecycle remains visibly separate from independently checked candle evidence. Contradictions, missing prefixes, incomplete closes, rule drift and stale captures remain explicit.

Edition 1.1.0 adds an optional `calendar` array aligned one-to-one with the OHLC rows: `[candle_open, previous_source_close, previously_projected_next_open]`, in Unix seconds. Butterfly freezes the preceding source candle's native next-opening projection. Both products accept a gap only when its previous close equals the last retained close and the actual opening exactly matches that projection. This handles a chart's scheduled closures without guessing weekends, exchange schedules or broker offsets. Missing metadata, unmatched timestamps and omitted candles cannot bridge a gap. Older messages without this field retain the adjacent-close check.

The projection supplies timing, never future prices. Its provenance is TradingView; this is a consistency check rather than an independent audit of the provider's holiday calendar or missing data. [TradingView documents future timestamp projections](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/time/) and [session-dependent bar boundaries](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/sessions/). Event qualification still uses closed candles and the same strict comparisons. GBOP reports both unsupported indicator events and events established in the capture but omitted from the indicator report.

The larger on-chart scan retains at most 512 assigned candles per context. Automatic focus requests the five mapped assigned timeframes so a realtime focus change has an available dataset; explicit focus requests only its assigned timeframe. An exported capture can therefore contain less evidence than the chart engine had. That can make a reported historical event unverified in GBOP; GBOP must request the missing chart evidence rather than replace it with MT5 data. The packet stays within [TradingView's 40,960-character alert limit](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000773947-alert-name-and-message-size-limits/), with an explicit guard against sending a truncated message.

## Limits and delivery status

- This is a closed-candle capture, not screen sharing, tick streaming or a permanent live link. It does not include manual chart drawings, other indicators, positions or fills.
- Paired SMT/boneless recognition and automatic Super Soup entry qualification are not implemented in Butterfly; the Super Soup range validity assessment is implemented. A local body check does not establish those conditions.
- Native daily/weekly/monthly session grids, closures and DST can limit independent reconstruction. The receiver does not silently rebucket those intervals.
- No capture is written into the MT5 candle store. The selected raw capture stays in the authenticated browser page and accompanies its voice requests; it is not persisted as market history.
- No live deployment, published script update, server alert or continuous webhook receiver was created during development.

Continuous delivery needs a separate authenticated, member-scoped receiver connected to the deployed GBOP service and a TradingView server alert configured to send to it. That receiver and alert lifecycle are not part of the implemented manual capture route. TradingView requires two-factor authentication for webhooks and cancels requests exceeding three seconds; credentials must not be embedded casually in alert messages. See [webhook setup](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000529348-how-to-configure-webhook-alerts/) and [credential guidance](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000722015-using-credentials-for-webhooks/). No existing broker bridge credential is reused.

## Edition 1.2.0 correction

Daily event names use supplied native trading-day dates, while exact opening/closing timestamps remain unchanged. Calendar witness rows accept `[opening, previousClose, projectedOpen, tradingDayUtcMidnight]`; legacy three-field rows remain readable without inventing a trading date. `soup_open` and `soup_at` optionally report a separately proven Super Soup. The reader checks those claims against the actual closed candles.

Model 1 body identity and CISD validation are separate. The panel and key callouts show Model 1, CISD and independently established Super Soup. Own-range invalidation is retained as hover/structured evidence; it does not erase Model 1 or substitute for Super Soup. Return is hover context only. Old rule editions retain their original declarations and produce an edition mismatch when reviewed against current rules.


## Edition 1.3.0 capture detail

An optional `butterfly.soup_follow_through` record reports cleanliness, parent function, wick-origin excursion, separate Model 1-boundary extension, adverse-close count, first re-purge/touch and target timestamps, coverage and terminal-candle upper-bound status. GBOP recomputes these against the captured candles and reports differences; it never trusts the reported interpretation alone. Missing earlier coverage cannot be replaced by a broker feed. Existing capture schemas remain readable, with their original rule identity preserved.
