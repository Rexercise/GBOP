Journal narration, management events and correction continuity

A story remains a private unfinished narrative until the member explicitly
requests finalization. Unknown prices, quantities, times, risk, currency and
results do not block capture. Reported facts are not independently verified fills.

Use one stable entry_index per reported position. entry_kind may be initial,
reentry or add_on. An initial position's partial exit and remaining runner are
management events on the same entry, not additional fills. Each event has a stable
event_index within that entry and kind partial_exit, runner_exit, exit or
stop_adjustment. Repeated mentions patch that identity rather than append another.
status distinguishes reported, planned, cancelled and unknown. Text fields retain
supplied objective, P/L, quantity, timing and notes without money conversion or
assumed units. Full values and correction history persist in the existing draft
JSON. No schema migration or performance/execution-row creation is introduced.

The brief review includes at most three entries and three events per entry, with
explicit omission counts. The complete story remains available on the same draft.
An explicit target replaces a contextual default. Direction's status follows the
known direction rather than retaining an obsolete missing flag. Correcting a
reported range interpretation does not erase verified price evidence for the
unchanged range. Superseded descriptions remain in correction history only.

Voice uses patient semantic turn detection by default; a valid configured
GBOP_VAD_EAGERNESS override is retained. Listen-first guidance waits through
self-correction and avoids repeated intake menus. Barge-in still cancels obsolete
speech while an already-running write is allowed to resolve with its real receipt.
These controls reduce premature replies; synthetic tests do not establish live
microphone quality or the cause of an earlier client disconnect.

Verification uses invented narratives, SQLite persistence/reconnect tests,
existing ownership/concurrency checks and voice interruption simulations. No
member journal is a test fixture. A live owner test remains a separate gate.

Rollback: preserve the compatible narrative reader/writer if new event-bearing
stories have been saved. Older releases reject unknown entry fields during
re-finalization, although the underlying JSON remains intact. Do not strip stored
events or rewrite historical journals to accommodate an older release.

Voice confirmation and extraction continuity
Explicit save authorization remains on the same owned draft when existing-record
identity reconciliation is required, including after reconnect. Resolving that
identity does not require repeating the save request. Explicit opt-out still
revokes permission; bare assent is not a save command. A pending separate-trade
question can be resolved from the member's explicit answer even when the model
omits the redundant flag. Finalization remains atomic with the canonical record.

Explicit custom titles and labeled direction confirmations survive extraction
omissions. A confirmed entry with an uncertain model is retained as reported entry
narration with unknown model, price, size, risk and result, never as a new execution
row. Repeated confirmation does not add entries. Reported lessons are retained
when structured context is omitted. A selected primary range can coexist with
other setups explicitly described as context; completing a previously unresolved
range identity is not treated as replacement of a verified range.

These changes do not migrate or correct existing journals. The original detailed
speech cannot be reconstructed from a relay summary that omitted it. Full capture
of parent dates, side order and countertrend reasoning is explicitly required in
voice guidance; actual acoustic completeness still requires a new supervised
voice test and saved-record comparison.
