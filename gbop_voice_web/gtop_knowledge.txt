TURTLE SOUP
[ICT FOUNDATION]
Turtle Soup is a liquidity-raid / false-break concept: price runs an old high, old low, or obvious liquidity and fails to sustain the breakout.

[GTOP REFINEMENT]
Within GTOP CRT sequencing, Turtle Soup is the earliest fundamental CRT entry and is associated with the manipulation/purge phase.

TURTLE WICK SOUP
[GTOP REFINEMENT]
A Turtle Wick Soup is a purge where liquidity is taken by the wick without the assigned-timeframe body establishing a close through that liquidity level.
GTOP commonly gives this lower probability and often manages it toward 50% of the range.

MODEL 1 CANDIDATE / BODY-CLOSE LOGIC
[GTOP REFINEMENT]
When a candle purges liquidity and its real body CLOSES THROUGH that liquidity, that candle becomes the MODEL 1 CANDIDATE candle.
That body close identifies the candidate. It does not itself confirm Model 1.

Two paths follow:

A) SUPER SOUP — PRE-CONFIRMATION
[GTOP EXCLUSIVE / GTOP REFINEMENT]
Before CSD confirmation, GTOP may Turtle Soup the Model 1 candidate candle itself on the correlated/assigned timeframe.
That is Super Soup.
Super Soup is an anticipatory / risk-entry framework.

B) MODEL 1 / CSD — CONFIRMED
[GTOP REFINEMENT]
Model 1 confirms only when price CLOSES THROUGH THE OTHER SIDE of that specific candidate candle's liquidity.
Once that happens, the change in state of delivery is confirmed and the Model 1 candle can be used in its intended function.

Sequence:
1. Purge external liquidity.
2. Body closes through that liquidity -> Model 1 candidate identified.
3. Before CSD -> Super Soup may be used as a risk entry.
4. CSD confirms when price closes through the opposite side of the candidate candle.
5. After CSD -> Model 1 function is active.

BREAKER BLOCK
[ICT FOUNDATION]
A Breaker Block is a failed order block that price violates and later uses from the opposite side on a retracement.
It is not merely a pullback.
The failed order block flips role and may act as support/resistance on the retest.

OTE — OPTIMAL TRADE ENTRY
[ICT FOUNDATION]
OTE is a Fibonacci retracement framework.
The broader OTE zone is approximately 62% to 79% of the dealing range, with 70.5% as the central / precise OTE level commonly referenced in ICT material.

[ROMEO / ACADEMIC CRT]
Breaker Block and OTE share the third CRT entry slot, but they are not the same setup.

KOD TURTLE SOUP
[ROMEO / ACADEMIC CRT + GTOP TERMINOLOGY]
Always refer to entry four as KOD Turtle Soup.
KOD is a Turtle Soup occurring later in the CRT sequence, typically the last Turtle Soup prior to the drawn liquidity / terminal objective.

[ICT FUNCTIONAL PARALLEL]

BLESSED THIEF
[GTOP EXCLUSIVE]
Blessed Thief is the GTOP-exclusive fifth CRT entry.

PRICE, NOT OPENING TIME
Blessed Thief is anchored to the OPENING PRICE of the selected candle.
It is NOT anchored to the clock-time moment when that candle first opens.
Execution can occur later when price returns to that opening price while manipulation is completing and the CRT thesis remains valid.

POST-MANIPULATION NATURE
Blessed Thief is a post-manipulation entry.
The level may be planned while price is manipulating, with the stop beyond the relevant manipulation extreme, but actual execution is intended after the manipulation has expressed itself and price revisits the chosen candle's opening price.

BIBLICAL / "CROSS" REFERENCE
[GTOP EXCLUSIVE]
The name Blessed Thief intentionally references the Blessed Thief on the cross, who received a last-minute blessing.

GTOP metaphor:
- the selected candle's opening price is "the cross,"
- price crosses / changes side around that open,
- price later revisits the opening-price cross,
- the trader catches the late / final blessing before distribution continues.
