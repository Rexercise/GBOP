DOUBLE BONELESS / BONELESS DOUBLE PURGE
[GTOP OWNER CLARIFICATION, REVISION 5]

Two distinct paired descriptors apply to any asset and any range play with a relevant correlated comparison: 9ate8, Young Lefty, GCT or others. Keep the underlying play. Boneless alone needs non-confirmation at one side.

DOUBLE BONELESS (V1)
The same asset purges neither parent-range boundary; it stays boneless on both sides. Its counterpart supplies the qualifying Double Purge. Preserve the original completed objective, then the opposing purge and required inside close on the selected parent timeframe. An opposing objective touch or lower-timeframe return alone is insufficient. Track later reversal delivery separately. Roles do not switch.

BONELESS DOUBLE PURGE (V2)
Different correlated assets purge the opposing boundaries of corresponding parent ranges. Each purges only one side and stays boneless at the side purged by its partner. Neither individually forms a Double Purge; the second purge is on the counterpart. Roles switch between sides. V1's individual Double Purge requirement does not apply to V2. These names are distinct, not interchangeable aliases.

Both retain existing Boneless qualification, parent-timeframe setup intervals and required closing confirmation. Minutes of separation alone are not non-confirmation. Incomplete intervals or missing paired candles remain unverified. Track each asset's physical purges and delivery; counterpart movement is not a local objective touch. No fixed buy-side-first or sell-side-first order, new entry, tier, automatic V6, automatic full-objective completion, guaranteed reversal or profit follows. Same-asset double purge alone is not Double Boneless; generic "double purge" is not an alias.

ONE CHART VS BOTH
V1: both purges appear on one chart; its counterpart has neither. V2: the two opposite-side purges appear across both charts, not together on either chart.
