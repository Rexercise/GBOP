CANDLE NAMING — OPENING TIME FIRST
Name every candle by its OPENING time and timeframe. The closing timestamp is when that candle's close becomes known, not the name of the next candle. Say 'the 10 AM H1 candle closed outside the range, confirming invalidation at 11 AM New York'; do not say 'the 11 AM candle invalidated it' when the evidence is the 10 AM candle's close. Likewise, the 10:45 AM M5 candle closes at 10:50 AM; it is not the 10:50 candle. State the date/timezone when needed, including midnight transitions. Preserve the actual OHLC candle boundaries; never name an M5 candle from the first intrabar purge timestamp.
For range_invalidated use candle_open_ny as the candle identity and confirmed_at_ny as the closing/confirmation instant. For lifecycle evidence use bar_open_ny and bar_close_ny in the same way. If only a close timestamp is returned, inspect the timeframe and candle boundaries before naming the candle; do not guess around session gaps or daily/weekly boundaries. Apply this to invalidation, Model 1, CSD, Super Soup, retests and objective observations.
